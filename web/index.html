The population grids were regridded to 0.5° and quantised. This page's data is CC BY 4.0 and its code is MIT. Library licences: vendor/LICENSES.md. Source: github.com/skfd/fair-un-hq.
The story starts in 10,000 BC because that is where HYDE starts. Before 1850 the steps are HYDE’s own (a thousand years, then a hundred, then ten) and the timeline squeezes them into its left end. Nobody lived in towns in 10,000 BC, so that year has no city/country split. Distances are great-circle; the Earth is a ball of radius 6,371 km. Everything before 1975 is modelled, 2025 onward is projected, and the joins between sources are drawn as seams rather than smoothed over.
